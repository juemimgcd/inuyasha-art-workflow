# Generation specification

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: standalone vertical manga illustration
Selected medium: black-and-white manga
Period mode: classic-balanced
Aspect ratio: 2:3 portrait

Primary request:
Create a new, text-free vertical black-and-white manga illustration of Inuyasha and Kikyo unexpectedly meeting again at an old shrine on a rainy night. This is a restrained emotional reunion, not a combat scene and not a copy of any source panel.

Identity ledger:
- 犬夜叉 | young half-demon form | very long light hair and two triangular dog ears on top of the head | compact youthful face, strong upper eyelids, bead necklace | loose fire-rat robe with wide sleeves and full hakama, barefoot | Tessaiga remains sheathed and visually secondary | slightly broader and more forward in the frame | do not borrow Sesshomaru's crescent, cheek markings, shoulder fur, armor, or swords.
- 桔梗 | young shrine maiden | very long straight black hair with blunt bangs and long side sections | restrained eyes, small mouth, calm but wounded expression | white kosode with broad sleeves and long hakama, period footwear | no raised weapon; bow may remain unobtrusive or outside the crop | slender, composed, standing under the shrine eaves | do not borrow Kagome's school uniform, backpack, modern shoes, or youthful schoolgirl cues.

Input images:
- Image 1: dominant manga rendering reference. Use only its economical facial construction, clean white skin, black-hair mass, limited halftone, restrained emotion, and selective background detail. Do not copy its panels, text, characters other than Kikyo, or story content.
- Image 2: supporting night composition reference. Use only its black tree canopy, upward night depth, quiet Inuyasha profile, and separation between dark environment and pale face. Do not reproduce its insect, lettering, panel borders, or original event.
- Image 3: official identity reference for Inuyasha's half-demon full-body construction.
- Image 4: official identity reference for Kikyo's shrine-maiden full-body construction.

Scene/backdrop:
An old mountain shrine after midnight in steady rain: dark wooden eaves across the upper frame, slick stone steps leading into depth, a rope or simple shrine silhouette kept understated, wet tree trunks and clustered foliage falling into large black masses, and thin pale rain lines cutting through the darkness. No modern objects.

Subject and exact moment:
They have just recognized each other after a long separation. Inuyasha has stopped in the open rain slightly closer to the viewer, body tense but hands lowered. Kikyo stands under the edge of the shrine roof several paces away, still and composed. They look directly at one another. Neither smiles, attacks, embraces, or speaks. The emotional center is the silent distance between them and the conflict between longing and restraint.

Composition/framing:
One complete vertical illustration, not a multi-panel page. Use a medium-wide two-shot showing both figures from about the knees upward or nearly full body. Let the roof eaves frame the top and the stone steps create a subtle depth line between them. Keep clear negative space and rain between their faces. Their eye line is the primary focal axis; Inuyasha's pale hair and Kikyo's black hair form the main black-white counterpoint. Keep the shrine and trees subordinate to the figures.

Medium construction:
Printed black-and-white supernatural manga made with flexible tapered dip-pen contours. Make outer silhouettes heavier than facial lines and fingers. Keep faces and skin mostly untouched paper white. Render Kikyo's hair as one strong black mass cut by narrow white highlight channels; render Inuyasha's light hair mostly white with a few tapered locks. Use broad garment folds rather than material rendering. Allow slight organic line variation rather than perfect digital-vector contours.

Value and tone plan:
Use four clear groups: paper white on faces and Inuyasha's hair; clean line art; one light and one middle dot-screen family for rain, garment separation, wet distance, and mist; unbroken black for the night canopy, roof underside, Kikyo's hair, and a few emotional accents. Keep at least one third of the image as clean white or very light negative space. Permit only one dense texture zone in the wet shrine background. Give each garment at most one screen tone.

Effects and setting:
Use straight and slightly wind-bent rain strokes, small white splashes on stone, sparse runoff from the eaves, and a faint mist edge. Keep all effects behind or around faces, dog ears, hands, clothing edges, and the bead necklace. No lightning, magical aura, action burst, or dramatic weapon effect.

Constraints and invariants:
Only Inuyasha and Kikyo appear. Preserve Inuyasha's half-demon dog ears, bead necklace, fire-rat clothing construction, bare feet, and sheathed sword. Preserve Kikyo's straight black hair, blunt bangs, shrine-maiden layers, and adult restrained presence. Keep them emotionally connected but physically separated. Render no lettering, speech bubbles, sound effects, logos, signatures, or watermarks.

Avoid:
Kagome's school uniform or modern era markers; Sesshomaru features; soft airbrush shading; glossy TV-anime cel lighting; colored anime converted to grayscale; painterly digital grayscale; photoreal faces, lips, pores, or 3D nose shadows; uniform vector outlines; halftone covering every surface; excessive crosshatching; cinematic bloom; random speed lines; extra characters; romance-poster glamour; combat poses; copying the source pages.
