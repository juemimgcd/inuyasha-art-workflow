# Candidate 01 image-generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: finished standalone black-and-white manga illustration

Primary request: Create a new manga illustration of Izayoi and child-form Inuyasha standing together in a Sengoku-period Japanese aristocratic residence. Izayoi must be on the LEFT. Child Inuyasha must be on the RIGHT. Both characters face straight toward the viewer, with their eyes looking forward.

Input images and strict authority:

- Image 1 is manga STYLE ONLY. Use only its flexible tapered dip-pen line hierarchy, black-and-white massing, solid black hair with narrow white highlight channels, restrained halftone, and clean white skin. Do not reproduce its dialogue, balloons, panel borders, framing, pose, characters, or story.
- Image 2 is IZAYOI IDENTITY ONLY. Preserve her adult human noblewoman identity, very long dark hair, soft blunt bangs, calm delicate face, layered wide-sleeved court kimono with restrained floral roundels and trailing hem. Do not turn her into Kikyo and do not give her shrine-maiden clothing.
- Image 3 is CHILD INUYASHA IDENTITY AND CHILD-FORM ONLY. Preserve a clear four-to-five-head-tall young child body, large rounded child head, narrow shoulders, short limbs, small hands and feet, long light hair, two dog ears on top of the head, rounded young face, wide cross-collar sleeves and loose hakama scaled to a child, barefoot. No Beads of Subjugation and no Tessaiga. Do not age him into a teen or adult.

Scene/backdrop: In front of the main hall and wooden engawa of a Sengoku-period Japanese noble residence, with wood pillars, shoji screens, a hanging blind, tatami interior, and a restrained glimpse of the garden. Credible simple architectural perspective; background detail stays lighter than the two figures.

Composition/framing: horizontal 3:2, eye-level frontal full-body two-shot. Izayoi occupies the left half and child Inuyasha occupies the right half. Both stand upright on the same ground plane, fully visible from hair and ears to feet and trailing hem. Keep clear space between their silhouettes so hair, sleeves, and limbs do not merge. Izayoi is naturally much taller; child Inuyasha reaches around her waist or lower chest. Both bodies and faces point directly toward the viewer, not three-quarter or profile.

Expression/mood: quiet, dignified maternal warmth. Izayoi has a gentle composed expression. Child Inuyasha is calm, slightly solemn, and unmistakably young.

Style/medium: classic balanced monochrome supernatural manga print. Flexible organic G-pen contours; outer silhouettes heavier than facial features; mostly white skin; solid black mass for Izayoi's hair; mostly white light hair for Inuyasha defined by contour and a few strands. Broad period-garment folds. Use at most one light and one middle dot screen, one tone family per garment, and pure black for selected focal accents. Keep at least one third of the image as white or very light negative space.

Spatial integrity: one coherent depth system, unambiguous front/behind overlap, both figures grounded, feet and trailing kimono resting correctly, no floating, no interpenetration, no fused sleeves, no duplicated limbs, no broken hands.

Constraints: exactly two focal characters; no attendants, warriors, servants, extra children, animals, or supernatural effects. No speech balloons, panel borders, captions, lettering, logo, signature, or watermark.

Avoid: full-color anime art desaturated to gray, uniform vector outlines, smooth digital gradients, cel shading, glossy highlights, painterly grayscale, photorealistic faces, dense gray everywhere, copied source composition, three-quarter-facing characters, adult-proportioned Inuyasha.
