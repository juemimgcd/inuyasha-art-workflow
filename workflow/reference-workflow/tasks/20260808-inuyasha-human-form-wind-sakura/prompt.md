Scene/backdrop:
A quiet open lawn in daylight, suggested with sparse tapered grass blades and a lightly toned distant horizon. Cherry blossom petals drift through the air from an off-frame tree. Keep the setting secondary and uncluttered.

Subject and exact moment:
Inuyasha in human form stands still immediately after putting Tessaiga away. The blade is completely inside its scabbard at his hip. One hand rests naturally near the sheathed sword while the other hangs relaxed. His expression is calm, alert, and slightly distant. A steady breeze moves his long black hair, wide sleeves, and hakama; the cherry petals follow the same wind direction.

Composition/framing:
Vertical 2:3 standalone full-body illustration, slightly low eye-level camera. Keep both bare feet visible on the grass. Build one elegant wind diagonal through the hair, sleeves, robe hem, and petals. Leave clean white negative space around the face and upper hair silhouette. The sheathed sword remains readable at the waist without dominating the character. Design this composition anew; do not imitate a reference panel.

Medium construction:
Each manga style-reference image controls mark-making only. Use its declared line hierarchy, black-white balance, halftone economy, and facial simplification; ignore and do not reproduce its characters, dialogue, balloons, panel borders, layout, or story. Classic-balanced black-and-white supernatural manga print: flexible tapered dip-pen contours, exterior silhouette heavier than facial features and fingers, slight organic line variation, compact youthful face, clean white skin, broad garment folds. Render the long black hair as the main pure-black mass cut by a few narrow white highlight channels. Use sparse pen lines for grass and petals.

Value/color plan:
Pure monochrome. Four value groups only: white paper and skin; clean line art; one light-to-middle dot screen on the robe and distant lawn; solid black for hair, beads, sword accents, and a few grounding shadows. Keep at least one third of the image as clean white negative space. No continuous grayscale painting.

Effects and setting:
Use a small number of clearly drawn cherry petals at varied depths, some sharp near the character and a few soft or lightly toned farther away. Hair, sleeves, grass tips, and petals must all indicate the same breeze. No speed-line explosion; the motion should feel quiet and lyrical.

Constraints and invariants:
One character only. Human form must be unmistakable: black hair, no dog ears, no claws, no fangs. Preserve prayer beads, fire-rat robe construction, loose hakama, bare feet, and a fully sheathed Tessaiga at the hip. Full body visible. Text-free: no speech balloons, captions, sound effects, logos, signatures, watermarks, or panel borders.

Avoid:
White hair, dog ears, half-demon features, Sesshomaru traits, drawn or exposed blade, duplicated sword, extra fingers, fused hand and hilt, modern clothing, shoes, extra characters, excessive cherry blossoms obscuring the face, copied source composition, anime cel shading, glossy highlights, painterly grayscale, photorealistic face, uniform vector outlines, dense gray over every surface, cinematic bloom, motion blur.
